Mathematics

{{ .Description }}

{{ len $mathResources }} resources across 7 topic branches — unit plans, worksheets, investigations, and games.

Mathematics on this site is not a collection of procedures. It is a set of questions worth arguing about. Every resource is designed around the principle that understanding is built — by students, through tasks that require genuine reasoning — not delivered through worked examples and then practised into submission.

The seven branches below are not syllabus headings. They are the conceptual territories where the most important mathematical ideas live for middle school learners: the meaning of number, the logic of shape, the power of pattern, the language of data. Each resource in each branch targets the deepest possible understanding of that territory, not the widest possible coverage of it.
