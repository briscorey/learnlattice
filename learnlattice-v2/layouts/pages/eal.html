For EAL Teachers Supporting MYP

🎯 Before the lesson

Print the word wall for the topic. Share the visual explainer link in Google Classroom as a 5-minute warm-up. Students arrive with the concept activated.

💡 During the lesson

Point to the word wall on the wall. When students write their investigation response, open the sentence frames on the board — they copy the frame and fill in the blanks.

📋 Assessment time

The sentence frames are mapped to MYP criteria A–D and differentiated by level. A student aiming for 5-6 gets different frames than one at 3-4. This is built-in differentiation.

🌏 Multilingual support

Word walls include Chinese (中文) and Korean (한국어) translations. Students see the English term alongside their first language. More languages coming.

Why This Exists

Most MYP resources assume English fluency. Instructions are dense. Hooks require reading comprehension before mathematical thinking can begin. The students with the strongest reasoning are often the last to start — because they are still decoding the language.

Every resource on LearnLattice is written for classrooms where students work in their second, third, or fourth language. Not as an afterthought. As the starting point.

01

The problem comes before the language

Every investigation starts with a visual, a number, or a physical action — not a paragraph. Students access the mathematics before they access the English.

02

Hooks work visually

The first thing students see is an image, a number, or an interactive — not a wall of text. "What is this?" is a question every student can engage with.

03

Sentence frames scaffold the writing

Understanding the maths is one challenge. Writing about it in English is a second, separate challenge. Sentence frames bridge that gap without lowering the cognitive demand.
