The story behind this site

I teach MYP mathematics and science at Nanjing International School. Thirteen years in classrooms — ten in Australian public schools, three in international education. I teach non-English speaking students using inquiry-based pedagogy.

I built LearnLattice because the resources I needed didn't exist: genuinely inquiry-based, properly aligned to MYP criteria, and designed for multilingual learners. What started as personal classroom materials became a platform with 222 resources spanning 12 MYP strands across 4 year levels, plus a student portal with 10 self-directed learning tools.

The technology is AI-assisted — I used Claude as a development partner to build the framework, the games, and the infrastructure. The pedagogy is mine. The classroom testing is mine. The voice is mine. This is what practitioner-led, AI-amplified resource development looks like.
