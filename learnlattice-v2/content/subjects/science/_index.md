## Free MYP Science Resources

LearnLattice provides over 100 free MYP science resources spanning biology, chemistry, physics, earth science, ecology, and space science. Every investigation is designed around the inquiry cycle that the IB MYP framework requires. students do not simply follow a method, they formulate questions, design experiments, process data, and evaluate their conclusions.

## Inquiry-Based, Not Recipe-Based

Traditional science worksheets tell students exactly what to do and what to expect. MYP science investigations should give students a question worth investigating and the space to design their own approach. Our resources balance structure with genuine inquiry: enough scaffolding that every student can begin, enough openness that the most capable students can extend the investigation in directions the teacher did not plan.

Every science investigation includes the practical details that make it work in a real classroom: equipment lists that use common school lab materials, safety considerations, realistic timelines for single and double lessons, and teacher notes explaining what to look for during the investigation.

## MYP Science Criteria

Science in the MYP is assessed across four criteria. [Criterion A](/assessment/criterion-a/) (Knowing and Understanding) assesses whether students can explain scientific concepts and apply them. [Criterion B](/assessment/criterion-b/) (Inquiring and Designing) assesses whether students can formulate questions, design experiments, and identify variables. [Criterion C](/assessment/criterion-c/) (Processing and Evaluating) assesses data handling, analysis, and evaluation of methods. [Criterion D](/assessment/criterion-d/) (Reflecting on the Impacts of Science) assesses whether students can connect science to society, ethics, and real-world implications.
