## Free MYP Biology Resources

LearnLattice offers 20 free MYP biology resources designed around genuine scientific inquiry. Students do not memorise diagrams. they observe living systems, design experiments, and build explanations from evidence.

### Topics Covered

Our biology resources span the core MYP topics: cell structure and function (including an [interactive Cell Explorer game](/resources/cell-explorer-game/)), body systems and homeostasis, ecology and ecosystems (food webs, biodiversity, human impact), genetics and inheritance, and adaptation and natural selection. Each investigation connects to MYP key concepts like Systems, Change, and Relationships.

### How These Resources Work in the Classroom

Every biology investigation includes a hook question that makes students curious before any content is introduced. For example, our [Dead or Alive](/resources/dead-or-alive-biology-investigation/) investigation asks students to classify borderline cases. is a seed alive? Is a virus?. before they learn the formal criteria for life. This builds conceptual understanding rather than vocabulary recall.

Investigations include teacher moves, common misconceptions, EAL scaffolds, and Criterion B/C alignment. Browse our [science assessment tools](/assessment/) for rubrics and annotated exemplars.
