## Free MYP Fractions and Ratios Resources

Fractions are where many students first decide whether they are "good at maths." Our resources build conceptual understanding through visual models and real contexts, so that fractions make sense before they become procedures.

### Topics Covered

Our resources cover fraction equivalence, operations with fractions, ratios and proportional reasoning, percentages and percentage change, and the connections between fractions, decimals, and ratios. The [Fraction Fighter game](/resources/fraction-fighter-game/) provides interactive practice, and [The Fairness Problem](/resources/the-fairness-problem-fractions-inquiry/) launches fraction thinking through a question every student has an opinion about: what does it mean to share fairly?

### Proportional Reasoning Across MYP

Proportional reasoning is one of the most important mathematical ideas in the MYP, connecting fractions to ratio, scale, probability, and data analysis. Our resources build these connections explicitly, so students see fractions not as an isolated topic but as a way of thinking that appears across mathematics and science.
