## Free MYP Geometry Resources

Geometry on LearnLattice is visual and hands-on. Students explore spatial relationships through construction, transformation, and proof — not just formula application.

### Topics Covered

Our geometry resources cover angle properties, coordinate geometry, transformations (reflection, rotation, translation), tessellations and symmetry, similarity and scale, circle geometry, and measurement (area, volume, surface area). The [Geometry Transformer game](/resources/geometry-transformer-game/) lets students explore transformations interactively, and [The Impossible Triangle](/resources/the-impossible-triangle/) introduces non-Euclidean thinking.

### Geometry as Investigation

The strongest MYP geometry tasks ask students to discover properties rather than apply given formulas. Our [Tessellation Explorer](/resources/grade-6-tessellation-explorer-investigation/) lets students investigate which polygons tessellate and why. The [Circle Theorems](/resources/grade-9-circle-theorems-investigation/) investigation guides students to discover angle relationships through construction before they see the formal proofs.
