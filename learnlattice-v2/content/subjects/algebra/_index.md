## Free MYP Algebra Resources

Algebra on LearnLattice is taught through pattern and generalisation, not symbol manipulation. Students discover algebraic relationships by investigating visual and numerical patterns before they are introduced to formal notation.

### Why Pattern-First Algebra Works

The IB MYP framework treats algebra as a way of expressing generalisations that arise from investigating patterns. Our resources follow this approach: students build staircases and count blocks ([The Staircase Problem](/resources/the-staircase-problem-algebra-investigation/)), explore growing shapes ([The Growing Squares](/resources/the-growing-squares/)), and discover that algebra is the language for describing what they have already noticed.

This approach is particularly effective for multilingual learners. The visual entry point means students engage with the mathematics before the English. Our [EAL scaffolds](/eal/) provide sentence starters at three levels for describing, explaining, and justifying algebraic thinking.

### Criterion B Investigations

Algebra is the natural home of [Criterion B: Investigating Patterns](/assessment/criterion-b/). Our algebra resources include complete assessment packs with tasks, mark schemes, and annotated student responses showing what levels 3-4, 5-6, and 7-8 look like in practice.
