## Free MYP Chemistry Resources

LearnLattice provides 20 free MYP chemistry investigations that put practical work at the centre of learning. Students do not read about reactions — they observe, measure, and explain them.

### Topics Covered

Our chemistry resources cover particle theory and states of matter, chemical reactions and conservation of mass, acids, bases and neutralisation, separation techniques, reaction rates, and combustion. Higher-level resources include electrolysis and atomic theory for MYP Year 4-5 students.

### Practical, Safe, and Classroom-Ready

Every chemistry investigation uses common school lab equipment and includes clear safety guidance. The [Disappearing Candle](/resources/the-disappearing-candle-chemistry-investigation/) investigation hooks students with an observable phenomenon before introducing combustion chemistry.

All resources are tagged with the MYP criteria they target. For assessment support, browse our [Criterion B assessment packs](/assessment/criterion-b/) which include chemistry-specific tasks with annotated student responses.
