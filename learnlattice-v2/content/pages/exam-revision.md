## Free MYP Revision Quizzes for Grade 6–8

LearnLattice revision quizzes are free, interactive review tools for IB Middle Years Programme Mathematics and Science. Each quiz covers a complete MYP unit with scaffolded questions, instant feedback, and full explanations. designed for focused revision before Criterion A assessments.

### Available Quizzes

**MYP Earth Science. Grade 6:** 40 questions across 5 stages covering Earth's layers, convection currents, plate tectonics, tectonic plate names, plate boundaries, continental drift, earthquakes, and volcanoes.

**MYP Forces & Motion. Grade 7:** 39 questions across 5 stages covering types of forces, net force, Newton's three laws, mass vs weight, gravity, simple machines, levers, and gears.

**MYP Geometry. Grade 6:** 29 procedurally generated questions across 5 stages covering perimeter, area, surface area, volume, and capacity of 2D and 3D shapes. with a searchable formula sheet and built-in calculator.

### How the Quizzes Work

Every quiz uses 5 scaffolded stages that build from recall to application to synthesis, mirroring the MYP Criterion A achievement level descriptors. You must complete each stage before unlocking the next. Wrong answers give a targeted hint addressing the specific misconception, then a full explanation. Six question types keep revision active: multiple choice, drag-and-drop, fill-in, true/false, matching, and ordering. No login or account is needed. just pick a topic and start.
