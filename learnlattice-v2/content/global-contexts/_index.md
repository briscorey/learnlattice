## Browse by Global Context

Global Contexts give MYP learning real-world relevance. They answer the question every student asks: *why are we learning this?* Every resource on LearnLattice is tagged with a Global Context, so you can build units that connect mathematics and science to the issues that matter.

The six MYP Global Contexts are: Scientific and Technical Innovation, Identities and Relationships, Orientation in Space and Time, Globalization and Sustainability, Fairness and Development, and Personal and Cultural Expression.
