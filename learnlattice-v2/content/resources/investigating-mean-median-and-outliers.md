## Overview

Students create datasets, add outliers, and investigate the effect on mean vs median. They discover when each measure is most appropriate.

## The Investigation

### Phase 1 — Explore
Students explore the initial problem through hands-on activity, collecting data systematically.

### Phase 2 — Organise and Analyse
Organise results in tables, look for patterns, calculate differences or ratios.

### Phase 3 — Generalise
Describe the pattern in words, then as a mathematical rule. Test with new cases.
