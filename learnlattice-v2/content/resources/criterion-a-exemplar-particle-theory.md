## The Task

This exemplar presents three student responses to the same Criterion A investigation, annotated at levels 3-4, 5-6, and 7-8.

---

## Student Response A — Achievement Level 3-4

Identifies basic features but cannot explain why. Description is qualitative. No mathematical or scientific reasoning beyond surface observations.

**Teaching note:** This student needs the question "How do you know?" asked repeatedly until they learn to support every claim with evidence.

---

## Student Response B — Achievement Level 5-6

Uses correct terminology and shows logical reasoning. Verifies findings against data. Explains patterns using subject-specific knowledge. May not yet distinguish between checking specific cases and proving a general rule.

**Teaching note:** This student is ready for the push from "I checked and it works" to "Here is why it must work."

---

## Student Response C — Achievement Level 7-8

Provides structural justification. Connects findings to underlying principles. Acknowledges limitations. Distinguishes between what the data shows and what it cannot show. Uses precise language throughout.

**Teaching note:** The key sentence in every Level 7 response is one that shows the student thinking about the *limits* of their own analysis.

---

## Using This Exemplar

1. **Before assessment:** Show Response A. Ask students what they would add. Then show B. Then C.
2. **After assessment:** Use the annotations to explain grades. Students see exactly what separates their work from the next level.
3. **For moderation:** Project these during a department meeting. Are your teachers applying the same standard?
