## Overview

Students design and conduct their own survey, investigating the best way to collect, organise, and display categorical and numerical data.

## The Task

Students work through a structured investigation or application task, building skills appropriate to MYP Year 1.

## Key Steps

### Step 1 — Introduction
Students encounter the problem through a concrete, engaging stimulus.

### Step 2 — Investigation / Application
Students work systematically, collecting data or applying mathematics/science to the real context.

### Step 3 — Analysis
Students organise findings, identify patterns or draw conclusions.

### Step 4 — Reflection
Students evaluate their work: Does it make sense? What could be improved?

## EAL Scaffolds
- Visual instructions alongside written text
- Sentence frames for every written response
- Key vocabulary pre-taught with images
- Partner discussion before individual writing

## Differentiation
- **Approaching**: Guided template with sentence starters
- **Meeting**: Scaffolded but student-directed
- **Exceeding**: Open extension with justification required
