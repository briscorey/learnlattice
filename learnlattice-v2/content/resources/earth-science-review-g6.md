## How to Use This Review

**Earth Science Review** is a 5-stage interactive revision activity covering everything from Earth's internal structure to the surface events driven by plate tectonics.

### The 5 Stages

**🌍 Stage 1. Earth's Layers**. Identify and describe the crust, mantle, outer core, and inner core. Properties, thickness, state of matter.

**🔥 Stage 2. The Convection Engine**. Explain how convection currents in the mantle drive plate movement. Density, heat transfer, circular flow.

**🗺️ Stage 3. Plate Boundaries**. Classify divergent, convergent, and transform boundaries. Predict surface effects at each type.

**🧩 Stage 4. Continental Drift**. Use evidence to support Wegener's theory. Fossils, rock matching, coastline fit, climate evidence.

**🔗 Stage 5. Putting It Together**. Connect interior processes to surface phenomena. Cause-effect chains from core heat to earthquakes and volcanoes.

### Question Types

This review uses 6 different question types to test your understanding in different ways: multiple choice, true/false, ordering, matching, fill-the-blank, and multi-select.

### MYP Connection

This review supports **Criterion A (Knowing and Understanding)** by testing recall, comprehension, and application of Earth Science concepts. Every wrong answer provides a targeted hint addressing the likely misconception, followed by a full explanation.

**Key Concept:** Change. Earth's interior drives continuous, slow change that reshapes the surface over millions of years.
