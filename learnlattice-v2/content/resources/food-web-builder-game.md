## How to Play
1. **Place organisms** — Click or drag organisms from the sidebar onto the canvas. Feeding links are created automatically based on natural prey relationships.
2. **Build the web** — Add producers (plants), primary consumers (herbivores), secondary consumers (small predators), and tertiary consumers (top predators). Watch the web grow.
3. **Remove a species** — Click the red "Remove Species" button, then click any organism to remove it from the ecosystem. The info panel shows which species lose food sources and which populations would collapse.
4. **Explore** — Try removing different species. Which removal causes the most damage? Which causes the least? Why?
## The Game
## Investigation Questions
1. Which trophic level has the most species in your web? Why do you think this is?
2. Remove a producer (like grass). How many species are affected? Now remove a tertiary consumer (like the hawk). How many are affected? Which removal is more damaging? Why?
3. The concept of a "keystone species" describes an organism whose removal causes disproportionate damage to the ecosystem. Can you identify a keystone species in this web?
## Classroom Use
Project the game and build the web as a class, discussing each organism's role. Then ask groups to predict what happens if a specific species is removed — vote before clicking. The surprise when a seemingly unimportant species collapses the web is a powerful teaching moment.
