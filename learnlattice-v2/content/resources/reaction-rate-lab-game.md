## How to Play
1. **Set the conditions** — Adjust temperature, concentration (number of particles), surface area (particle size), and catalyst
2. **Run the experiment** — Click "Run Experiment" to start a 10-second simulation
3. **Watch the particles** — Red particles are reactants, yellow are the other reactant. When they collide with enough energy, they turn green (reacted)
4. **Read the data** — Collisions, successful reactions, reaction rate, and average speed update in real time
5. **Read the insight** — After each run, the lab explains what happened and why

Which factor had the biggest effect? Why? Use particle theory to explain each result.
## Classroom Use
This simulation makes collision theory concrete. Students can SEE that higher temperature means faster-moving particles, that more particles means more collisions, and that catalysts don't change speed — they lower the energy needed for a collision to succeed. The visual is the explanation.
Pairs with: "The Disappearing Candle" chemistry investigation.
