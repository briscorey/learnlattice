## How to Play

1. **Choose a difficulty level** — Easy (single operations), Medium (two operations or squaring), Hard (cubic, absolute value, piecewise)
2. **Feed numbers in** — Click any number on the pad to send it through the machine
3. **Watch what comes out** — The output appears below. Your test history builds on the right
4. **Guess the rule** — When you think you know it, type it in the guess box (e.g., `x × 2 + 1`)
5. **Build your streak** — Solve consecutive rules to build a streak. Can you identify them in just 2 tests?

## Classroom Use

The Function Machine is ideal for introducing algebraic notation. Students play on individual devices for 5-10 minutes, then the class discusses: what strategies work best for guessing rules? Key insight: testing with 0 and 1 reveals the structure of most rules immediately. This is the beginning of systematic mathematical thinking.

**MYP Criterion B connection:** The game IS a criterion B investigation — students are identifying patterns, describing rules, and generalising from specific cases.
