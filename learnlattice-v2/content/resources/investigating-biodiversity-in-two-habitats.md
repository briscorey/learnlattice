## Overview

Students use quadrats and transects to compare species richness and abundance between two habitats, calculating Simpson's diversity index.

## The Investigation

### Research Question
Students formulate a testable research question and identify independent, dependent, and controlled variables.

### Method Design
Students design a fair test, select appropriate equipment, plan data collection, and consider safety.

### Data Collection
Students carry out the experiment, recording quantitative data in appropriate tables with units and repeats.

### Processing and Analysis
Students calculate averages, create appropriate graphs, identify trends, and draw evidence-based conclusions.

### Evaluation
Students evaluate the reliability of their data, identify sources of error, and suggest improvements.

## Differentiation
- **Approaching**: Given research question and method, focus on data collection and graphing
- **Meeting**: Design own method from a given research question
- **Exceeding**: Formulate own question, full experimental design, critical evaluation
