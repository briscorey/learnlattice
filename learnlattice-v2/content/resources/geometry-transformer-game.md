## How to Play

1. **Choose a shape** — Triangle, Square, or L-Shape
2. **Translate** — Slide the X and Y sliders to move the shape across the grid
3. **Rotate** — Turn the shape around its centre (in 15° steps)
4. **Reflect** — Flip across the X-axis or Y-axis
5. **Enlarge** — Scale the shape from 0.5× to 3×
6. **Read the coordinates** — Every vertex shows its transformed position in real time

## Investigation Questions

1. Translate the triangle by (3, 2). What happens to each coordinate? Write a general rule.
2. Rotate the square by 90°. Which coordinates swap? Why?
3. Reflect the L-shape in the Y-axis, then translate it by (4, 0). Now reset and translate first, then reflect. Do you get the same result? What does this tell you about the order of transformations?

## Classroom Use

Project this and give students transformation instructions to follow: "Translate by (2, 3), then rotate 90°, then reflect in the X-axis." Students predict the final position BEFORE you apply the last transformation. The visual feedback is immediate — right or wrong, they see it instantly.
