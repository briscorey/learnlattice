## How to Play

1. **Drag components** from the sidebar onto the canvas — batteries, bulbs, resistors, switches
2. **Wire them together** — click the Wire tool, then click two terminals (the coloured dots) to connect them
3. **Complete the circuit** — when current can flow in a complete loop, the readings update and bulbs glow
4. **Experiment** — toggle switches, add resistors, build series vs parallel configurations

## Challenges

**Challenge 1:** Light a single bulb. Connect a battery to a bulb with two wires.

**Challenge 2:** Build a series circuit with two bulbs. What happens to brightness when you add the second bulb?

**Challenge 3:** Build a parallel circuit with two bulbs. Compare brightness to the series circuit. Why is it different?

## Classroom Use

Pairs with: "The Circuit Detective" investigation. Students can test their circuit designs in the simulator before building physical circuits. The real-time readings (voltage, current, resistance) make Ohm's Law concrete — change a resistor and watch the current change instantly.
