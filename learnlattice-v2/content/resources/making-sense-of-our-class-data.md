## Overview

Students collect data about their own class, calculate averages, create displays, and make meaningful comparisons to published statistics.

## The Task

Students work through a structured investigation or application task, building skills appropriate to MYP Year 1.

## Key Steps

### Step 1 — Introduction
Students encounter the problem through a concrete, engaging stimulus.

### Step 2 — Investigation / Application
Students work systematically, collecting data or applying mathematics/science to the real context.

### Step 3 — Analysis
Students organise findings, identify patterns or draw conclusions.

### Step 4 — Reflection
Students evaluate their work: Does it make sense? What could be improved?
