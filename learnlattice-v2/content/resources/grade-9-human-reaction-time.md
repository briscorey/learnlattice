## Overview

Students measure their own reaction times using a ruler drop test, then analyse class data for patterns by age, hand dominance, and practice.

## Learning Objectives

Students will develop skills in observation, pattern recognition, and mathematical/scientific reasoning through structured investigation.

## Classroom Use

This resource is designed for a single lesson (50-60 minutes) and works well with random grouping at whiteboards.
