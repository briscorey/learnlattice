A 4-stage circuits game building from components to troubleshooting:

1. **Components** — Identify batteries, bulbs, switches, ammeters, voltmeters, resistors, fuses
2. **Complete** — Determine if circuits are complete, series, parallel, or short circuits
3. **Predict** — What happens to brightness when you add bulbs in series vs parallel?
4. **Troubleshoot** — Diagnose why a circuit isn't working and suggest the fix
