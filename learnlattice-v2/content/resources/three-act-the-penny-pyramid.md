## Act 1: The Hook

You have two options:

**Option A:** Receive $1,000,000 today.

**Option B:** Receive 1 cent today, and the amount doubles every day for 30 days.

> **Which would you choose?** Write your choice and your reasoning. Vote as a class.

---

## Act 2: Calculate

| Day | Amount (cents) | Amount ($) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | $0.01 |
| 2 | 2 | $0.02 |
| 3 | 4 | $0.04 |
| 5 | 16 | $0.16 |
| 10 | 512 | $5.12 |
| 15 | 16,384 | $163.84 |
| 20 | 524,288 | $5,242.88 |
| 25 | 16,777,216 | $167,772.16 |
| 30 | ? | ? |

> **The formula:** Amount on day n = 2^(n-1) cents.

Day 30 = 2²⁹ = **536,870,912 cents = $5,368,709.12**

---

## Act 3: The Reveal

The doubling penny is worth **over $5.3 million** after 30 days. more than 5 times the million dollars.

> **But here is the shocking part:** On day 20, the penny is only worth $5,242. You would have waited 20 days watching your penny grow to a fraction of the million-dollar option. Exponential growth is invisible until it is not.

---

## The Sequel

- On what day does the penny first exceed $1,000,000? (Day 28: 2²⁷ = $1,342,177.28)
- What if the amount tripled instead of doubled? How would the result change?
- **Compound interest:** If you invest $1,000 at 7% annual return, how long until it doubles? (Rule of 72: approximately 72 ÷ 7 ≈ 10 years)
- **Population growth:** If a bacteria colony doubles every 20 minutes, starting from 1 bacterium, how many after 24 hours?
- **Viral spread:** If one person infects 2 others per day, how many days until 1 million people are infected? Why do epidemiologists worry about exponential growth?

---

## Teaching Notes

**3-Act Structure:** Act 1 creates a genuine choice (most students pick the million). Act 2 generates the data. Act 3 reveals the surprise and connects to real applications.

**MYP Criterion D Connection:** The sequel questions apply exponential growth to finance, biology, and epidemiology. genuine real-world contexts where mathematical literacy has personal and societal consequences.

**MYP Criterion B Connection:** Students investigate the pattern of doubling, describe it algebraically (2^n), and can justify the formula.

**Global Context. Sustainability:** Exponential growth is central to understanding climate change, resource depletion, and population dynamics. This task builds the mathematical intuition needed for those discussions.

**Key Teaching Moment:** The gap between Day 20 ($5,242) and Day 30 ($5.3 million) is the most powerful illustration of exponential growth available. Draw it on the board. Let students feel how flat the curve looks for 20 days, then how it explodes.
