## Overview

Students measure the rate of a chemical reaction by collecting gas over time, investigating the effect of concentration.

## Safety
- Follow standard laboratory safety procedures
- Wear safety goggles where required
- Report any spills or breakages immediately

## Equipment
Standard laboratory equipment as specified in the method.

## Method

### Setup
Prepare equipment and materials according to the procedure.

### Data Collection
Follow the step-by-step procedure, recording measurements in the data table provided. Complete at least 3 repeats for each condition.

### Data Processing
- Calculate averages for repeated measurements
- Create an appropriate graph (line graph for continuous data, bar chart for categorical)
- Identify trends and anomalies

## Analysis Questions
1. What pattern does your data show?
2. Does your data support or refute your hypothesis? Explain using specific data points.
3. Identify one source of error in your experiment. How could you reduce this error?
4. If you repeated this experiment, what would you change and why?
