## Overview

Students compare aerial photographs of a location over 20 years to quantify habitat loss and discuss the ecological consequences.

## Learning Objectives

Students will develop skills in observation, data collection, pattern recognition, and evidence-based reasoning through structured investigation.

## Classroom Use

Designed for 50-60 minutes. Works with random grouping at vertical whiteboards (BTC practices).
