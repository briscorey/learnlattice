## Overview

This worksheet extends the [Grade 7 Proportional Reasoning Unit](/resources/grade-7-proportional-reasoning-unit/) with structured practice across ratios, unit rates, proportions, percent change, and scale. Every section uses authentic data or real-world context so students practice applying — not just computing.

---

## Section A — Ratios

**A1.** Write each ratio in simplest form. Show what you divided by.

a) 18:24 b) 45:30 c) 120:80 d) 14:21:35

**A2.** A concrete mix uses 3 parts cement, 5 parts sand, and 7 parts gravel.

a) Write the ratio cement:sand:gravel.
b) What fraction of the mix is cement?
c) To make 120 kg of concrete, how many kg of each material is needed?

**A3. Part-to-part vs part-to-whole.**
A bag contains 8 red marbles and 12 blue marbles.
a) Write the ratio of red to blue.
b) What fraction of the bag is red?
c) If you add 4 more red marbles, what is the new ratio of red to blue? Is this the same ratio as before?

---

## Section B — Unit Rates

**B1.** Find the unit rate for each situation.

a) 360 km in 4.5 hours → ___ km/h
b) $7.92 for 12 pens → $___ per pen
c) 840 words typed in 14 minutes → ___ words per minute
d) 2.4 L of paint covers 36 m² → ___ m² per litre

**B2. Better value comparison.**

| Product | Amount | Price |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Orange juice A | 1.25 L | $3.50 |
| Orange juice B | 2 L | $5.20 |
| Orange juice C | 750 mL | $2.40 |

Calculate the price per 100 mL for each. Which is the best value? Show full working.

**B3. Real context.**
Felix Baumgartner's freefall covered approximately 36,500 m in 4 minutes 20 seconds before he opened his parachute.

a) Convert 4 minutes 20 seconds to seconds.
b) Calculate his average speed during freefall in m/s.
c) Convert this to km/h.
d) The speed of sound is approximately 343 m/s at sea level. Was he faster or slower than sound? By how much?

## Section C — Proportions

**C1.** Solve each proportion. Show your method.

a) 3/5 = x/35 b) 8/x = 12/21 c) x/9 = 14/6 d) 2.5/4 = x/10

**C2. Proportional or not?**

Decide whether each table shows a proportional relationship. Show how you know (calculate y/x for each row).

Table 1:
| x | 2 | 5 | 8 | 11 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 6 | 15 | 24 | 33 |

Table 2:
| x | 1 | 3 | 6 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 5 | 9 | 15 | 23 |

**C3. Map scale.**
A map has a scale of 1:250,000.

a) A river on the map is 7.4 cm long. How long is the real river in km?
b) Two cities are 38 km apart in reality. How far apart are they on the map in cm?
c) A student says: "A 1:50,000 map shows a smaller area than a 1:250,000 map." Is this correct? Explain.

---

## Section D — Percent Change

**D1.** Calculate the percent change. State whether it's an increase or decrease.

a) From 80 to 100 b) From 150 to 120 c) From 46 to 69 d) From $12.50 to $9.75

**D2. The symmetry trap.**
A share price increases 25% on Monday, then decreases 25% on Tuesday.

a) If the starting price was $80, what is the price after Monday?
b) What is the price after Tuesday?
c) Is it back to $80? If not, what is the percentage change from start to finish?
d) Explain in one sentence why a 25% increase and 25% decrease don't cancel out.

**D3. Real context — population data.**

| Country | Population 2000 | Population 2020 |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Japan | 126,920,000 | 125,710,000 |
| Nigeria | 122,280,000 | 206,150,000 |
| Germany | 81,900,000 | 83,160,000 |

a) Calculate the percent change in population for each country.
b) Which country had the greatest absolute change? Which had the greatest relative (percent) change?
c) Japan's population is declining. At the same rate of change, estimate Japan's population in 2040. (Assume the same percent change per 20 years continues.)

---

## Section E — Mixed Challenge

**E1.** A builder is scaling up a deck design. The original design is 3.2 m × 4.8 m.
a) She wants to scale the design by a factor of 1.5. What are the new dimensions?
b) What is the area of the original deck? The new deck?
c) By what factor did the area increase? Why is this different from the scale factor of 1.5?

**E2. Reflection.**
Two students are comparing test scores:
- Student A: improved from 40 to 60 marks.
- Student B: improved from 70 to 84 marks.

Ahmed says A improved more because the difference is 20 marks vs 14 marks.
Bianca says B improved more because 70 is a better starting point.

Using percent change, decide who made the greater improvement *relative to where they started*. Do you think percent change is always the fairest way to compare? Explain your reasoning.

## Answers (Teacher Version)

**A1:** a) 3:4 b) 3:2 c) 3:2 d) 2:3:5
**A2:** a) 3:5:7 b) 3/15 = 1/5 c) cement 24kg, sand 40kg, gravel 56kg
**A3:** a) 8:12 = 2:3 b) 8/20 = 2/5 c) 12:12 = 1:1 — no, different ratio

**B1:** a) 80 km/h b) $0.66 c) 60 wpm d) 15 m²/L
**B2:** A=$0.28/100mL, B=$0.26/100mL, C=$0.32/100mL → B is best value
**B3:** a) 260 s b) 36,500÷260 = 140.4 m/s c) 140.4×3.6 = 505.4 km/h d) Slower — 343−140.4 = 202.6 m/s slower at sea level (note: at altitude, speed of sound is lower, so he did break it)

**C1:** a) 21 b) 14 c) 21 d) 6.25
**C2:** Table 1: y/x = 3 for all → proportional. Table 2: y/x varies (5, 3, 2.5, 2.3) → not proportional
**C3:** a) 7.4×250,000 = 1,850,000 cm = 18.5 km b) 38km = 3,800,000 cm ÷ 250,000 = 15.2 cm c) Incorrect — 1:50,000 is zoomed in more, shows a smaller area in more detail

**D1:** a) +25% b) −20% c) +50% d) −22%
**D2:** a) $100 b) $75 c) Not back — net change = (75−80)/80 × 100 = −6.25% d) The 25% decrease is calculated on the higher post-increase price, so it removes more dollars than the increase added
**D3:** a) Japan −0.95%, Nigeria +68.6%, Germany +1.54% b) Nigeria greatest absolute and relative c) Japan ~124,510,000 (applying −0.95% again)

**E1:** a) 4.8m × 7.2m b) 15.36 m², 34.56 m² c) area increased by factor of 2.25 (= 1.5²) — area scales as the square of the linear scale factor
**E2:** A: (20/40)×100 = 50%; B: (14/70)×100 = 20%. A improved more relative to starting point. Percent change isn't always fairest — context matters (starting from a very low base inflates the percent).
