## How to Play

**MYP Quest** is a platformer where every level is generated based on the MYP topic you choose. Each time you play, the levels are different.

### Controls

- **Arrow keys** or **WASD** to move
- **Up / W / Space** to jump
- Collect ⭐ stars for points
- Avoid 🔺 spikes
- Reach the 🚪 exit portal to complete the level

### Themes

Choose from 8 MYP topics — each one changes the visual style and difficulty progression:

- **Algebra Land** — equations, patterns, and variables
- **Cell World** — organelles, membranes, and microscopic life
- **Force Fields** — gravity, friction, and Newton's laws
- **Eco Realm** — ecosystems, food chains, and habitats
- **Chance Canyon** — probability, randomness, and outcomes
- **Reaction Zone** — elements, reactions, and particle theory
- **Energy Peaks** — kinetic, potential, and thermal energy
- **Cosmic Voyage** — planets, stars, and the universe

Each level displays an educational fact about the current topic. Levels get harder as you progress — more platforms to navigate, more hazards to avoid, and trickier jumps.
