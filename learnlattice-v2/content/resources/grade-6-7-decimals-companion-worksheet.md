## How to Use This Worksheet

This worksheet builds from place value understanding through to multi-step operations. Work through the sections in order — each one builds on the last. Show all working; answers without method earn no credit.

---

## Section A — Place Value

**A1. Write the value of the underlined digit.**

| Number | Underlined digit | Value |
|--------|-----------------|-------|
| 5.**3**7 | 3 | ___________ |
| 0.0**4**2 | 4 | ___________ |
| 12.**0**5 | 0 | ___________ |
| 8.9**7**3 | 7 | ___________ |
| **1**4.06 | 1 | ___________ |

**A2. Write in expanded form.**

a) 6.408 = ___________________________________________

b) 0.073 = ___________________________________________

c) 25.305 = ___________________________________________

**A3. Write in standard decimal form.**

a) 3 + 0.4 + 0.06 + 0.002 = ___________

b) 10 + 7 + 9/100 = ___________

c) Four and sixty-three thousandths = ___________

## Section B — Ordering and Comparing

**B1. Circle the larger decimal. Show your reasoning.**

a) 0.8 **or** 0.75 — reasoning: ___________________________________________

b) 3.04 **or** 3.4 — reasoning: ___________________________________________

c) 0.099 **or** 0.1 — reasoning: ___________________________________________

**B2. Order these decimals from smallest to largest.**

0.5, 0.51, 0.049, 0.509, 0.5090, 0.05

Smallest → Largest: _________________________________________

**B3. Real data problem.**

The top 5 finishers in the 400m hurdles at a school athletics carnival:

| Place | Athlete | Time (seconds) |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| ? | Hamid | 58.47 |
| ? | Sofia | 57.9 |
| ? | James | 58.4 |
| ? | Priya | 57.93 |
| ? | Leo | 58.09 |

a) Rank them 1st to 5th. Write the correct place for each athlete.

b) By how many seconds did 1st place beat 2nd place? Show your calculation.

c) If Sofia's time improved by 0.15 seconds in the next race, what would her new time be?

b) 9.0 − 3.7 = 5.7 — ___________________________________________

c) 0.6 + 0.04 = 0.10 — ___________________________________________

**C3. Real-world context.**

Marcus is training for a triathlon:
- Swimming: 0.75 km
- Cycling: 18.4 km
- Running: 5.25 km

a) What is the total race distance?

b) After 2.8 km of running, how far is he from finishing the run leg?

c) His training target for the week is 80 km total. So far he has completed 4 full triathlon distances and an extra 6.35 km cycling. How much more does he need to train?

## Section E — Division

**E1. Calculate.**

a) 8.4 ÷ 4 = ___________

b) 6.3 ÷ 0.7 = ___________

c) 15 ÷ 0.3 = ___________

d) 0.48 ÷ 0.06 = ___________

**E2. Step-by-step guide: dividing by a decimal.**

Fill in the blanks:

To calculate 7.2 ÷ 0.4:
1. Multiply both numbers by _____ to make the divisor a whole number.
2. This gives _____ ÷ _____.
3. Answer: _____

To calculate 0.36 ÷ 0.04:
1. Multiply both numbers by _____ to make the divisor a whole number.
2. This gives _____ ÷ _____.
3. Answer: _____

**E3. Real-world context.**

A 1.68 m length of ribbon is cut into pieces that are each 0.12 m long.

a) How many pieces are cut? Show your calculation.

b) If instead the ribbon is cut into 8 equal pieces, how long is each piece?

c) Which cut — (a) or (b) — produces longer individual pieces? How much longer?

---

## Section F — Mixed Challenge

**F1. Multi-step problem.**

A family's electricity bill shows:
- Peak usage: 4.7 kWh per day at $0.28 per kWh
- Off-peak usage: 8.3 kWh per day at $0.11 per kWh
- Daily supply charge: $0.95

a) Calculate the daily cost for peak usage.

b) Calculate the daily cost for off-peak usage.

c) What is the total daily bill including the supply charge?

d) What is the 30-day (monthly) cost?

e) They install solar panels which reduce peak usage by 2.4 kWh per day. How much do they save per month?

**F2. Explain your thinking.**

A student says: "Dividing by 0.5 gives the same answer as multiplying by 2."

a) Test this claim with two examples.

b) Explain *why* this works using what you know about decimals and fractions.
