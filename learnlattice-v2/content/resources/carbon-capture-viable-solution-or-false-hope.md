## Overview

Students investigate carbon capture technologies, evaluate their chemical feasibility and scalability, and assess whether they can meaningfully address climate change.

## The Task

A rigorous investigation or evaluation task appropriate for upper MYP students.

## Structure

### Phase 1 — Engage
Students encounter the problem through a challenging stimulus that requires higher-order thinking.

### Phase 2 — Investigate / Analyse
Students work through the core task with increasing independence, making decisions about approach and method.

### Phase 3 — Synthesise
Students draw conclusions, evaluate their approach, and connect to broader themes.

### Phase 4 — Communicate
Students present their findings with appropriate mathematical/scientific language and justification.

## EAL Scaffolds
- Academic vocabulary pre-taught with context examples
- Structured frameworks for extended writing
- Peer discussion protocols before individual work

## Differentiation
- **Meeting**: Structured investigation with guidance at key decision points
- **Exceeding**: Open-ended with multiple valid approaches, requires justification
