## Overview

Students use algebra to model the cost of a school trip with fixed and variable costs, determine break-even points, and recommend pricing to the school council.

## The Investigation

### The Context
Students encounter the real-world problem and identify which mathematical tools are needed.

### Setting Up the Model
Translate the real situation into mathematical language: variables, equations, diagrams.

### Solving and Interpreting
Apply mathematical skills to find solutions, then interpret what the numbers mean in context.

### Reflecting
Evaluate: Does the answer make sense? What are the limitations? How could the model be improved?

## Criterion Alignment

| Strand | What Students Do |
|--------|-----------------|
| Identify the real-world problem | Identify the real-world problem and relevant mathematical content |
| Select appropriate mathematics/science | Select and set up appropriate mathematical models |
| Apply to the context | Apply mathematics accurately and interpret results in context |
| Reflect on reasonableness | Evaluate the reasonableness of the solution and discuss limitations |

## EAL Scaffolds

- **Sentence frame**: 'The mathematics shows that... This makes sense because... However, this model doesn't account for...'
- **Word bank**: model, estimate, approximate, reasonable, limitation, assumption, context

## Differentiation
- **Approaching**: Guided setup with provided variables and equations
- **Meeting**: Open modelling with scaffolded reflection prompts
- **Exceeding**: Compare multiple models, evaluate trade-offs, present recommendations
