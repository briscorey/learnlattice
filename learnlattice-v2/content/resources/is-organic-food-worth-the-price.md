## Overview

Students investigate pesticide chemistry, compare organic vs conventional farming methods, and evaluate health and environmental claims.

## The Investigation

### The Issue
Students encounter the real-world issue through a stimulus (news article, data set, scenario) and identify the relevant science.

### Scientific Investigation
Students research the underlying science, gathering evidence from multiple sources and evaluating their reliability.

### Evaluating Perspectives
Students identify different stakeholder perspectives, evaluate the strength of scientific evidence on each side, and consider ethical dimensions.

### Forming a Position
Students develop an evidence-based position, acknowledging limitations and trade-offs, and communicate their reasoning clearly.

### Reflection
Students reflect on how science interacts with society, culture, and ethics in this context, and consider what additional evidence would strengthen their position.
