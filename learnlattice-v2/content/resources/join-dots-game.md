## How to Play

**Join Dots** is Connect 4 with a twist: you have to answer a question correctly to earn your move. Get it wrong, and the AI plays instead.

Pick your topic from the tabs on the right — you can focus on algebra, probability, forces, cells, or any other MYP topic. Or leave it on "All Topics" for a mixed challenge.

### Rules

1. Choose a difficulty level for the AI opponent
2. Answer the question correctly to earn your move
3. Click any column to drop your piece
4. Connect 4 in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win
5. Get a question wrong? The AI gets a free move!

### Strategy Tips

- **Control the centre**: the middle column gives you the most winning opportunities
- **Think ahead**: sometimes blocking the AI is more important than building your own line
- **Choose your topic wisely**: pick a topic you're confident in when the game is close!
