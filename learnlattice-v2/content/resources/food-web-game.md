## How to Play
1. **Select organisms** to place in your food web — producers, consumers, and apex predators
2. **Draw connections** by clicking organisms to link predator → prey relationships
3. **Complete each round** by building the correct web for that ecosystem
4. **Read the debrief** — each round ends with the key ecological concept explained

## The Game
Six rounds, each teaching a distinct concept in ecosystem ecology:

| Round | Concept |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Food chains — linear energy transfer |
| 2 | Trophic levels and the 10% rule |
| 3 | Web redundancy and ecosystem resilience |
| 4 | Apex predators and trophic cascades |
| 5 | Omnivores and multi-level feeding |
| 6 | Complete forest web — all concepts combined |

## Classroom Use
Pairs with: "Understand Food Webs" and the "Invisible Thread" ecology investigation. Use this game before the investigation to build conceptual vocabulary — students arrive already knowing what trophic cascade means when they encounter it in data.

**MYP Criterion A:** Can you correctly identify feeding relationships and explain the direction of energy flow?

**MYP Criterion B:** What makes a food web more or less resilient? How would you design an investigation to test this?
