## 🔄 Patterns

Regularities that can be observed, described, and generalised. In mathematics, patterns are the foundation of algebra and number theory. In science, patterns emerge in periodic behaviour, classification systems, and experimental data.

**Example statement of inquiry:** *Recognising patterns allows us to make predictions and develop general rules.*
