## 📈 Change

Transformation over time or conditions. In mathematics, change appears as rates, sequences, growth, and decay. In science, change drives chemical reactions, evolution, geological processes, and energy transfer.

**Example statement of inquiry:** *Measuring and modelling change helps us understand both natural processes and human impact.*
