## ⚙️ Systems

Collections of interacting parts that form a whole. In mathematics, systems include simultaneous equations and coordinate systems. In science, systems range from cells and ecosystems to circuits and the solar system.

**Example statement of inquiry:** *Analysing the components of a system reveals how changing one part affects the whole.*
