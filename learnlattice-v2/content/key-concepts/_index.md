## Browse by Key Concept

The IB Middle Years Programme organises learning around Key Concepts. big ideas that transcend individual topics and connect learning across subjects. Every resource on LearnLattice is mapped to at least one Key Concept, so you can plan units that are genuinely concept-driven rather than topic-driven.

Key Concepts in MYP mathematics and science include Relationships, Systems, Logic, Change, Form, and Patterns. Click any concept below to see all resources that develop understanding of that idea.
