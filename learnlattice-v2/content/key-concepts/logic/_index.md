## 🧠 Logic

Reasoning, proof, and justified argument. In mathematics, logic underpins algebraic manipulation, geometric proof, and mathematical justification. In science, it drives hypothesis testing, controlled experiments, and evidence-based conclusions.

**Example statement of inquiry:** *Logical reasoning allows us to move from observation to justified conclusion.*
