Unit Conversion

Converting between mm, cm, m, and km using powers of 10.

The Chain

mm →÷10→ cm →÷100→ m →÷1000→ km

Example 1: 3,500 mm to metres

3500 mm ÷ 10 = 350 cm
350 cm ÷ 100 = 3.5 m

Example 2: 0.45 km to centimetres

0.45 km × 1000 = 450 m
450 m × 100 = 45,000 cm

The Rule

Going to smaller units → multiply (more of a smaller unit)
Going to bigger units → divide (fewer of a bigger unit)
