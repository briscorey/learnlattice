Mean from a Frequency Table

Multiply each value by its frequency, add the products, divide by total frequency.

The Data

Students were asked how many siblings they have:

SiblingsFrequency
05
112
28
33
42

Step 1: Multiply value × frequency

0×5=0, 1×12=12, 2×8=16, 3×3=9, 4×2=8

Step 2: Add the products

0 + 12 + 16 + 9 + 8 = 45

Step 3: Add the frequencies

5 + 12 + 8 + 3 + 2 = 30

Step 4: Divide

Mean = 45 ÷ 30 = 1.5 siblings

Key insight: You cannot just add 0+1+2+3+4 and divide by 5. The frequencies matter.
