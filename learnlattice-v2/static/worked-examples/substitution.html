Substituting into a Formula

Replace letters with numbers, then evaluate step by step.

Example 1: v = u + at

Find v when u = 5, a = 3, t = 4

v = 5 + 3 × 4
v = 5 + 12 = 17

Order matters: Multiply before adding (BIDMAS).

Example 2: A = πr²

Find A when r = 7

A = π × 7² = π × 49 = 153.9

Common mistake: (πr)² is wrong. Square r first, THEN multiply by π.

Example 3: E = ½mv²

Find E when m = 2, v = 6

E = ½ × 2 × 6² = ½ × 2 × 36 = 36 J
