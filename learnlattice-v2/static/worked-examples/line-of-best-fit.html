Drawing a Line of Best Fit

A straight line through the middle of scattered data points.

Step 1: Plot all data points

Plot each (x, y) pair as a dot. Do NOT connect them.

Step 2: Look at the overall trend

Going up = positive. Going down = negative. Scattered = no correlation.

Step 3: Draw the line

Use a ruler. The line should pass through the middle of the cloud with roughly equal points above and below.

Step 4: Check for anomalies

Any point dramatically far from the line might be an anomaly.

Common mistake: Connecting the first and last point. A line of best fit represents the overall trend, not point-to-point.
