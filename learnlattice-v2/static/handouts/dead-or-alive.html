Dead or Alive?
Biology · Grade 6-8
learnlattice.org
  
Is fire alive? It grows, consumes fuel, responds to wind.
1Write the 7 characteristics of life (MRS GREN).
2Apply each to FIRE: Yes, No, or Maybe?
3Repeat for: a virus, a seed, a robot.
4Verdict for each: Alive, Not Alive, Uncertain.
5DISCUSS: Is the definition of life a fact or invention?
