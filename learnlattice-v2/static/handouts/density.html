An orange floats in water. Peel it, and it sinks. Why?

1

Predict

Will it float or sink? Write predictions for each object.

2

Measure

Find mass and volume. Calculate density = mass / volume.

3

Test

Place each in water. Were your predictions correct?

4

Explain

Why does peeling change whether it floats? Use the word density.

5

Apply

Ships are steel, denser than water. Why do they float?
