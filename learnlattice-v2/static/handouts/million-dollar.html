The Million Dollar Question
Mathematics · Grade 7-8
learnlattice.org
  
$1,000,000. One dollar per second. How long?
1Estimate how long it would take to spend one million dollars at one dollar per second.
2Calculate the exact answer. Convert to a sensible unit.
3How much would one million dollars weigh in $1 coins? (One coin ≈ 8g)
4Convert to kilograms. Could you carry it?
