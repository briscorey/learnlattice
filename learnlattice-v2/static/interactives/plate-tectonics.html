Convergent Boundary
Plates push together
When two plates collide, the crust crumples upward forming fold mountains. Enormous pressure builds and is released as earthquakes.
Real examples: Himalayas (India + Eurasia), Alps (Africa + Europe)
Earth's surface is made of ~15 tectonic plates that float on the mantle. They move 2–10 cm per year — about as fast as your fingernails grow.
Tectonic plateTectonic plate: A massive slab of Earth's crust and upper mantle that moves slowlyMantleMantle: The hot, semi-liquid rock layer beneath the crustSubductionSubduction: When one plate slides beneath another into the mantlePangaeaPangaea: The supercontinent that existed 200 million years ago before the plates drifted apart
Mount Everest grows about 4mm per year because the Indian plate is still pushing into the Eurasian plate. It's been doing this for 50 million years!
