Algebra

Core Rules
Solving one-step
ax = b → x = b ÷ a
Divide both sides by the coefficient
Solving two-step
ax + b = c → x = (c − b) ÷ a
Subtract first, then divide
Distributive law
a(b + c) = ab + ac
Expand brackets by multiplying each term
Linear equation
y = mx + c
m = gradient c = y-intercept
🔢

Number

Percentages & Ratio
Percentage of amount
% × amount ÷ 100
e.g. 25% of 80 = 25 × 80 ÷ 100 = 20
Percentage change
(change ÷ original) × 100
Increase or decrease divided by the starting value
Fraction → Decimal
a/b = a ÷ b
e.g. 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75
Ratio simplification
a : b → (a÷HCF) : (b÷HCF)
Divide both parts by the highest common factor

Geometry & Measurement

Area
Rectangle
A = l × w
l = length w = width
Triangle
A = ½ × b × h
b = base h = perpendicular height
Circle
A = πr²
r = radius π ≈ 3.14159
Trapezium
A = ½(a + b) × h
a, b = parallel sides h = height
Perimeter & Circumference
Rectangle
P = 2(l + w)
Circle
C = 2πr = πd
d = diameter
Volume
Cuboid
V = l × w × h
Cylinder
V = πr²h
Prism
V = area of cross-section × length
Angles & Pythagoras
Angles in a triangle
a + b + c = 180°
Pythagoras' theorem
a² + b² = c²
c is the hypotenuse (longest side)

Statistics & Probability

Averages
Mean
Mean = sum of values ÷ number of values
Median
Middle value (when data is in order)
Mode
Most frequent value
Range
Range = highest − lowest
Probability
Basic probability
P(event) = favourable ÷ total
Complement
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)

Physics

Forces & Motion
Speed
speed = distance ÷ time
m/s or km/h
Weight
W = m × g
W = weight (N) m = mass (kg) g = 10 N/kg
Pressure
P = F ÷ A
P = pressure (Pa) F = force (N) A = area (m²)
Density
ρ = m ÷ V
ρ = density m = mass V = volume
Energy
Kinetic energy
KE = ½mv²
m = mass v = velocity
GPE
GPE = mgh
m = mass g = 10 h = height
Efficiency
efficiency = useful ÷ total × 100%
Power
P = E ÷ t
P = power (W) E = energy (J) t = time (s)
Waves
Wave speed
v = f × λ
f = frequency (Hz) λ = wavelength (m)
Frequency
f = 1 ÷ T
T = period (s)

Chemistry

Key Equations
Photosynthesis
CO₂ + H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂
Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (needs light)
Respiration
C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy
Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy
pH scale
0 ←acid— 7 —alkali→ 14
pH 7 = neutral. Lower = more acidic. Higher = more alkaline.
Conservation of mass
Mass of reactants = Mass of products
Atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed

Biology

Key Facts
Cell structure
Cell membrane → Cytoplasm → Nucleus
All cells have these three. Plants also have: cell wall, chloroplasts, vacuole.
Food chain energy
Producer → Primary → Secondary → Tertiary
Energy decreases at each level (~10% transferred)
Magnification
M = image size ÷ actual size
Used when drawing or measuring cells under a microscope
Genetics ratio
Punnett square → 3:1 or 1:1
3:1 ratio when both parents are heterozygous (Aa × Aa)
