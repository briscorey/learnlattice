Percent means "per hundred." So 25% of 200 means 25 out of every 100 — that's 50.
PercentagePercentage: A number out of 100. 'Per cent' literally means 'per hundred'MultiplierMultiplier: The number you multiply by: 1.2 for 20% increase, 0.8 for 20% decreasePercentage pointPercentage point: The difference between two percentages: 30% to 35% is 5 percentage points
Banks use compound percentages — interest on interest. At 7% annual interest, your money doubles in about 10 years (the Rule of 72).
