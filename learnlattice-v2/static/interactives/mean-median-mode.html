Add some numbers to get started. Mean = total ÷ count. Median = middle value. Mode = most common.
MeanMean: Total ÷ count. Affected by every value.MedianMedian: The middle value when sorted. Resistant to outliers.ModeMode: The most common value. Can be more than one.OutlierOutlier: A value far from the rest that can skew the mean
The 'average' salary is often reported as the median, not the mean, because a few billionaires would skew the mean way up!
