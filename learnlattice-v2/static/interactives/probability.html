Does Probability Work?

Flip the coin. Watch the results. The more you flip, the closer it gets to 50/50.

50.0% heads
The theoretical probability of heads is exactly 0.5. The experimental probability is what you actually get. With more flips, they converge.

Casinos make money because of tiny probability edges. A roulette wheel gives the house just a 2.7% advantage — but over millions of spins, that's billions of dollars.
