The rule: If an object's density is less than water (1.0 g/cm³), it floats. If more, it sinks.

density = mass ÷ volume
DensityDensity: Mass per unit volume: ρ = m/v. Units: g/cm³ or kg/m³MassMass: Amount of matter in an object (grams, kilograms)VolumeVolume: Amount of space an object takes up (cm³, m³, mL)DisplacementDisplacement: Measuring volume by how much water rises when an object is submerged
Osmium is the densest natural element at 22.6 g/cm³ — a coffee mug made of osmium would weigh about 2.5 kg!
