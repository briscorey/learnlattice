Coordinates are written as (x, y). The x-value tells you how far across. The y-value tells you how far up. The centre is called the origin (0, 0).
OriginOrigin: The point (0,0) where the axes crossQuadrantQuadrant: One of the four sections the axes divide the grid intox-coordinatex-coordinate: The first number — how far across (horizontal)y-coordinatey-coordinate: The second number — how far up or down (vertical)
